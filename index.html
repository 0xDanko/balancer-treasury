⚠ Important: The capital in these wallets is allocated for funding operations and protocol development. This capital should not be considered freely distributable to BAL holders — it is earmarked for sustaining and growing the protocol. The base Treasury NAV above is a more conservative measure of per-token backing.

Methodology

The Base NAV prices all assets in the DAO Treasury and DAO Multisig wallets — including DeFi positions (Aave, Lido, Compound, Maker), staked tokens (stkAAVE, stkGHO, wstETH, vlAURA), and governance tokens from treasury swaps — then divides by BAL circulating supply. Native BAL is excluded to avoid circular valuation.

Base NAV = (Treasury Value − Native BAL) ÷ Circulating Supply
Extended NAV = (Treasury + OpCo + Onchain − All Native BAL) ÷ Circulating Supply

Data sourced across Ethereum, Gnosis Chain, Arbitrum, Base, and other L2s. Token prices fetched live from CoinGecko API. Token symbols verified via CoinGecko contract addresses.

⚠ Balances are approximate. Complex DeFi positions may not be fully captured. See Karpatkey, Octav, and DeBank for authoritative data.
