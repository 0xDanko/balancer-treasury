Methodology

Data sources: Zerion API (via Cloudflare Worker proxy) for token balances, DeFi positions and USD prices. Etherscan for on-chain BAL supply. Where no source covers a chain or a contract, the dashboard falls back to the attested address list (measured on-chain 18 September 2026, DefiLlama prices) and labels the row as such.

Distributable NAV = Σ non-BAL value of every available distributable bucket ÷ Circ. Supply
Circ. Supply = totalSupply() − BAL held by every DAO-controlled address − tetuBAL permalocked

Tiers. Distributable is what the DAO could send to the main Treasury and distribute today. Time-locked value exists but is not yet available and is excluded from the headline NAV until its unlock date. Operating is the OpCo account, which is partly committed spend and is never blended into the headline.

BAL exclusion is fractional. Native BAL is excluded outright. Anything that resolves into BAL — the 80/20 BAL/WETH pool token, rAURA and auraBAL — is exited proportionally, so only its ETH leg (20.45% of value) counts toward NAV.

Double counting. Wallet and DeFi positions are fetched separately per bucket and merged with receipt-token dedup, so a wrapped position is never counted twice. The v3 buffers are held by the Vault contract rather than the Treasury Safe, which is why they need their own bucket.
