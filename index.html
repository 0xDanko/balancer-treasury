Methodology

This dashboard calculates the Net Asset Value (NAV) per BAL token by pricing all assets held across the Balancer DAO Treasury wallets — including DeFi positions (Aave, Lido, Compound, Maker, Balancer LP), staked tokens (stkAAVE, stkGHO, wstETH, vlAURA), and governance tokens from treasury swaps — then dividing by BAL circulating supply.

NAV per BAL = (Total Treasury USD Value − Native BAL Holdings) ÷ Circulating Supply

Native BAL is excluded from the numerator to avoid circular valuation. Treasury data is sourced from the DAO Treasury Multisig (0x0EFcCBb9...32fc89) and the DAO Multisig (0x10A19e7e...2e4f) across Ethereum, Gnosis Chain, Arbitrum, and Polygon. Token prices are fetched live from CoinGecko.

If BAL market price < NAV → the token trades at a discount to NAV (potentially undervalued relative to treasury). If BAL market price > NAV → trades at a premium.

⚠ Treasury balances are approximate, based on Karpatkey's 2025 Annual Review ($13.7M managed, ~$14.3M total balance sheet), DefiLlama ($14.6M), and on-chain data from Octav and DeBank. Some complex DeFi positions (nested LPs, veBAL locks) may not be fully reflected. Always refer to Karpatkey's official reports and Octav dashboard for authoritative data.
